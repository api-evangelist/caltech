On May 29, 2026, a crowd of about 150 people gathered in and around a colonial-style house on campus that belongs to Caltech's theater arts group, also known as TACIT . In one corner, visitors sat captivated by an attendee who claimed to have arrived from a future where whales have gone extinct. The group huddled together around her hand-drawn book of animals that no longer graced the world.
