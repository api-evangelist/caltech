Two missions led by Caltech and the Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL), which Caltech manages for NASA, have received recognition from NASA for outstanding achievements. Caltech's Jamie Bock , the Marvin L. Goldberger Professor of Physics and JPL senior research scientist, is receiving NASA's Exceptional Scientific Achievement Medal for SPHEREx (Spectro-Photometer for the History of the Universe, Epoch of Reionization and Ices Explorer).
