NASA announced today the selection of a new space telescope that will explore the cold, dusty universe in far‑infrared light, providing the ability to view cosmic objects and events at wavelengths that are otherwise invisible with existing telescopes. The Jet Propulsion Laboratory , which Caltech manages for NASA, will develop and operate the $1 billion observatory named PRIMA (the Probe far-Infrared Mission for Astrophysics).
