On October 8, the U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) announced a second round of funding for its Genesis Mission , a nationwide initiative to use AI to tackle pressing challenges in science and technology. Among the projects that will receive funding is a Caltech effort that will use AI to help identify new and better catalysts, materials that speed up chemical reactions.
